# Tone Review Output Format

Apply every rule from the Tone Check instructions above, then return your answer in exactly this format and nothing else:

HEAT: <number from 1 to 5>/5, <three to six word description>
CHANGES:
- <what you softened or changed, and the phrase it came from>
- <one bullet per change, at most five>
===REWRITE===
<the rewritten text only>

Heat scale: 1 = calm and professional, 2 = a little terse, 3 = noticeably irritated, 4 = angry, 5 = do not send.
If nothing needed changing, write "- No changes needed" under CHANGES.
Bullets should be short and plain. No em-dashes anywhere.
